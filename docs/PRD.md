## 1. Executive Summary & Product Overview

**CareerAI** is a production-oriented, full-stack, AI-driven career acceleration platform designed to empower job seekers in optimizing their application assets, evaluating alignment with target job descriptions, preparing for technical and behavioral interviews, and tracking job applications throughout their hiring lifecycle.

By leveraging Google Gemini models paired with deterministic parsing and schema-validated structured output, CareerAI transforms raw resumes (PDF, DOCX, TXT) into actionable intelligence:
- Quantitative section-by-section resume quality audit.
- Semantic job-fit analysis and skill gap identification.
- Tailored mock interview question generation and AI-evaluated answer feedback.
- Unified application pipeline tracking.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Important Disclaimer on AI Match Scoring:**  
> Matching scores and alignment metrics generated by CareerAI reflect semantic keyword overlap, structural alignment, and competency relevance. **They do not guarantee interviews, employment offers, or hiring probability.** CareerAI is an assistive advisory tool designed to enhance application quality.

## 2. Problem Statement

The modern job market presents acute challenges for applicants:
1. **Opaque Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS):** Candidates lack visibility into how automated parsers extract and score their resumes, leading to high rejection rates due to formatting or keyword mismatches.
2. **Generic, Non-Actionable Feedback:** Job seekers rarely receive specific, section-by-section guidance on quantifiable bullet points, leadership verbiage, or missing technical proficiencies.
3. **Misalignment with Job Descriptions:** Candidates struggle to systematically map their experience against specific job postings, missing critical domain requirements or unverified skills.
4. **Disconnected Interview Preparation:** Practicing for interviews is typically decoupled from the specific resume and job posting, leading to generic preparation that fails to address specific experience gaps.
5. **Fragmented Tooling:** Applicants juggle disparate platforms for resume building, job tracking, and interview preparation, creating friction and lost context.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

CareerAI resolves these problems through an integrated, privacy-focused platform:
- **Deterministic Text Extraction & Parsing:** Reliable extraction from PDF, DOCX, and TXT files without loss of structural context.
- **Server-Side AI Analytics (Google Gemini):** Deep semantic evaluation producing structured, schema-validated feedback on impact, clarity, brevity, and skill coverage.
- **Bi-Directional Job Match Engine:** Comprehensive alignment scoring against job descriptions, identifying explicitly matched skills, missing competencies, and experience discrepancies.
- **Contextual Interview Simulator:** Dynamic generation of technical, behavioral, and situational questions derived directly from the user's resume and target job role, including AI evaluation of user-submitted answers.
- **Built-in Application Tracker:** Centralized Kanban/table pipeline tracking application status, match scores, company notes, and interview dates.

## 7. Functional Requirements (FR)

### Module 1: Authentication & Account Management
- **FR-AUTH-001:** Users must be able to register using valid email, password (min 8 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special char), and full name.
- **FR-AUTH-002:** System must hash passwords using bcrypt (salt rounds $\ge 10$) prior to persistence.
- **FR-AUTH-003:** Authentication must use secure, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Strict` cookie-based JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
- **FR-AUTH-004:** Users must be able to securely log in, refresh sessions, and log out (invalidating tokens).
- **FR-AUTH-005:** Users must be able to update their profile (name, headline, target job title, preferences).
- **FR-AUTH-006:** Users must be able to permanently delete their account and all associated data (cascading deletion of resumes, analyses, job matches, interviews, and applications).

### Module 2: Resume Ingestion & Parsing
- **FR-RES-001:** System must support file uploads in PDF (`.pdf`), Microsoft Word (`.docx`), and plain text (`.txt`) formats up to 5 MB.
- **FR-RES-002:** System must extract plain text, contact info, detected sections (Education, Experience, Skills, Projects, Certifications), and raw text while preserving logical reading order.
- **FR-RES-003:** System must reject corrupt, encrypted, or unsupported file formats with descriptive client-safe error messages.
- **FR-RES-004:** Users must be able to list, preview extracted text, and delete uploaded resumes.

### Module 3: AI Resume Analysis & Quality Scoring
- **FR-AI-001:** System must send extracted resume text to Google Gemini using structured prompt templates and enforce strict Zod schema validation on the response.
- **FR-AI-002:** The analysis must calculate an Overall Quality Score (0–100) and 5 category sub-scores:
  1. *Impact & Quantifiable Metrics* (0–100)
  2. *Brevity & Formatting* (0–100)
  3. *Action Verbs & Style* (0–100)
  4. *Technical & Soft Skills Coverage* (0–100)
  5. *ATS Readability & Section Structure* (0–100)
- **FR-AI-003:** System must provide line-by-line bullet improvement suggestions (Original $\to$ Critique $\to$ Recommended Rewrite).
- **FR-AI-004:** Analysis results must be persisted and viewable in chronological history.

### Module 4: Job Description Matching & Skill Gap Analysis
- **FR-JOB-001:** Users must be able to submit a job description (raw text, title, company, URL optional) alongside a selected uploaded resume.
- **FR-JOB-002:** System must compute an Overall Alignment Score (0–100, explicitly marked as semantic match score, not guaranteed hiring probability).
- **FR-JOB-003:** System must extract and categorize:
  - *Matched Skills & Keywords* (explicitly found in both).
  - *Missing Skills & Keywords* (present in job description, absent in resume).
  - *Unverified / Weakly Positioned Skills* (implied or mentioned without evidence/impact).
- **FR-JOB-004:** System must provide tactical resume tailoring recommendations specific to the target role.

### Module 5: Contextual AI Interview Preparation & Evaluation
- **FR-INT-001:** System must generate 5–10 customized interview questions based on the candidate's resume and (optional) target job description.
- **FR-INT-002:** Questions must be categorized into Technical, Behavioral (STAR method), and Situational/Role-Specific.
- **FR-INT-003:** Users must be able to submit written answers for any generated question.
- **FR-INT-004:** System must evaluate answers using Gemini, returning an Answer Quality Score (0–100), Strengths, Weaknesses, Missing Key Concepts, and an Exemplary Model Answer.

### Module 6: Job Application Tracker & Analytics Dashboard
- **FR-APP-001:** Users must be able to create, view, update, and delete job applications with statuses: `Saved`, `Applied`, `Interviewing`, `Offered`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`.
- **FR-APP-002:** Applications can be linked to a specific Resume and Job Analysis record.
- **FR-APP-003:** Dashboard must present aggregated statistics: total resumes analyzed, average quality score, total applications by status, and recent activity timeline.

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

### 8.1 Performance (NFR-PERF)
- **NFR-PERF-001:** Standard REST API endpoints must respond in $< 200\text{ ms}$ (p95) under normal load.
- **NFR-PERF-002:** Resume parsing & AI analysis completion time must not exceed $15\text{ seconds}$ for a standard 2-page document.
- **NFR-PERF-003:** Database queries must utilize covered indexes for user-scoped queries.

### 8.2 Security & Privacy (NFR-SEC)
- **NFR-SEC-001:** Passwords must be hashed using bcrypt ($10\text{ salt rounds}$).
- **NFR-SEC-002:** JWT tokens must be stored exclusively in `HttpOnly`, `Secure` (in prod), `SameSite=Strict` cookies.
- **NFR-SEC-003:** All endpoints must enforce strict user ownership validation (IDOR protection: users cannot view, edit, or delete another user's resources).
- **NFR-SEC-004:** Rate limiting must be enforced globally ($100\text{ req}/15\text{ min}$) and strictly on AI generation endpoints ($10\text{ req}/5\text{ min}$) and authentication routes ($5\text{ req}/15\text{ min}$).
- **NFR-SEC-005:** File uploads must validate MIME types and file magic bytes, rejecting executable or disguised binaries.

### 8.3 Reliability & Fault Tolerance (NFR-REL)
- **NFR-REL-001:** Gemini API calls must implement exponential backoff retry (up to 3 attempts) for transient 429/503 errors.
- **NFR-REL-002:** If Gemini structured output fails Zod validation, system must log the raw output and return a clean error without crashing.

### 8.4 Usability & Accessibility (NFR-A11Y)
- **NFR-A11Y-001:** Frontend must comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards (color contrast $\ge 4.5:1$, keyboard navigability, semantic HTML, ARIA attributes).
- **NFR-A11Y-002:** User interfaces must provide explicit loading skeletons, progress indicators, empty states, and dismissible toast notifications.

## 9. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

### US-01: Resume Upload and Instant Analysis
*As an active job seeker,*  
*I want to upload my PDF/Word resume and receive instant feedback,*  
*So that I can identify formatting flaws and weak bullet points before applying.*
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a valid PDF file under 5 MB, when uploaded, the system parses text and sections without crashing.
  - The user sees an overall score (0–100) and 5 category breakdowns within 15 seconds.
  - At least 3 specific bullet-point rewrite suggestions are rendered with side-by-side comparison.

### US-02: Target Job Description Matching
*As a candidate applying for a specific role,*  
*I want to paste the job description alongside my resume,*  
*So that I can clearly see which required skills I have and which are missing.*
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a selected resume and a job description text, the system generates a match analysis.
  - Matched skills and missing skills are rendered in distinct color-coded badge groups.
  - Clear disclaimer is displayed: "Score indicates semantic skill alignment, not guaranteed hiring outcome."

### US-03: AI Interview Simulation & Evaluation
*As an applicant preparing for an upcoming interview,*  
*I want tailored interview questions generated from my resume and job description, and feedback on my answers,*  
*So that I can improve my interview performance.*
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - System generates at least 5 relevant questions with technical and behavioral tags.
  - When user submits an answer, AI provides a score (0–100), key strengths, areas for improvement, and an ideal model answer.

### US-04: User Data Privacy & 1-Click Deletion
*As a privacy-conscious user,*  
*I want to permanently delete my account and all uploaded resumes,*  
*So that my personal contact information and employment history are not retained.*
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Account settings page provides a "Delete Account & Data" action with double-confirmation dialog.
  - Upon confirmation, user record, all resumes, analyses, job matches, interview sessions, and applications are permanently deleted from MongoDB.
  - Auth cookie is cleared and user is redirected to the landing page.

## 10. Risk Analysis & Mitigation Strategies

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Hallucination / Unstructured Output** | High | Medium | Enforce strict Zod schema validation on JSON responses from Gemini; discard and retry invalid payloads. |
| **Gemini API Rate Limiting / Quota Limits** | Medium | Medium | Implement server-side token bucket rate limiting per user, request caching, and retry with exponential backoff. |
| **Malicious File Uploads** | High | Low | Validate MIME types, limit file size to 5MB, store parsed text only, and sanitize file metadata. |
| **User Misinterpreting Match Score as Guaranteed Job** | Medium | High | Display persistent UI disclaimers and educational tooltips explaining the semantic nature of match scores. |
| **IDOR / Unauthorized Data Access** | Critical | Low | Enforce strict user ID scoping (`{ _id: resourceId, userId: req.user.id }`) across all database queries in middleware/controllers. |

## 11. Unresolved Decisions & Assumptions

1. **Storage of Raw Files vs. Extracted Text Only:**  
   *Assumption for MVP:* In MVP, we store parsed text and document metadata in MongoDB to avoid external S3/cloud storage dependency and simplify data deletion. Future phases can integrate AWS S3/GCS with signed URLs.
2. **AI Provider Fallbacks:**  
   *Assumption:* Primary AI provider is Google Gemini (`gemini-1.5-flash` / `gemini-1.5-pro` or equivalent latest stable). Secondary provider fallback (e.g., Anthropic/OpenAI) is deferred to post-MVP.
3. **Email Verification:**  
   *Assumption for MVP:* Registration directly creates an active account to minimize onboarding friction for MVP testing. Production roadmap includes transactional email verification (SendGrid/Resend).
